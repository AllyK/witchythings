Witchy Things

Rituals, moon work, and dreamwork. A small portal of practices, kept honestly.

Everything here follows the same two commitments. First, each practice says plainly where it comes from: what is inherited from a named tradition or source, and what is invented as a personal framework. Second, only the practitioner decides what any of it means. Nothing on these pages is a verdict.
